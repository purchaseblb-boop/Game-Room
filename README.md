🎮 Game‑Room




Pause, play, and connect – all in one place.

Game‑Room is a fully functional, single‑file web app that brings together real‑time group video calling, text chat, a multi‑game arcade, and a rich note‑taking tool – completely free and ready to use.

Whether you're catching up with friends, collaborating on ideas, or just taking a break, Game‑Room has you covered.

🧩 What’s inside:
📞 3‑person group calls – crystal‑clear audio and video with screen sharing.

💬 Party chat – send messages and voice notes while you talk.

🕹️ Arcade – play 7 classic games instantly (Flappy Bird, Dino Run, Tic‑Tac‑Toe, and more).

📝 Notes – write rich text or draw, with pinned notes, search, and dark mode.

🔒 Private rooms – share your unique room code and invite anyone.

No sign‑ups, no installations – just open the page and go.
Perfect for game nights, study groups, or casual hangouts.
